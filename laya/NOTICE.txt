Switchcraft local model (Laya, round 3)
======================================

A layout/language decision model for Switchcraft: given a line typed on the keyboard and its readings
in the installed layouts (English, Ukrainian, Russian), it answers which reading each word was meant
in and which language the line is in. It is served on this PC by `laya-serve` (Laya 0.4.1); nothing
typed leaves the computer.

Made from:
- Encoder: jhu-clsp/mmBERT-base — https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/mmBERT-base — MIT License.
- Training and serving code: Laya 0.4.1 by ConvAI Innovations — https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya
  — Apache License 2.0.
- Training sentences: Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR, turned into wrong-layout typing
  examples by Switchcraft's own generator.

Files: model.safetensors (weights), encoder/ and tokenizer/ (mmBERT configuration and tokenizer),
questions.json, rl_agent_config.json, train_report.json (98.4% accuracy on its evaluation set).
